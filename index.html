FedReg Intel

FedReg Intel is a YouTube channel that explains the Federal Register, the daily journal of the U.S. government, in plain English, and helps people take part in federal rulemaking.

What we publish

  • Federal Register Daily: a weekday briefing on new rules, proposed rules and presidential documents, with links to the official text and to open comment periods.
  • Rulemaking 101: explainers on the Administrative Procedure Act, how rules are made, proposed and final rules, and effective dates.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@FedRegIntel

About the "FedReg Intel" app

The FedReg Intel app is the channel owner's own publishing tool. It signs in only to the FedReg Intel YouTube channel to upload episodes, set thumbnails and descriptions, and post a comment with links to the official documents. It is not offered to the public. See the privacy policy.

Videos use an AI-generated presenter and synthetic narration. They are informational summaries, not legal advice. Always check the official document on federalregister.gov.
