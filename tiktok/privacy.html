FEDREG INTEL · TIKTOK DRAFT UPLOADER

Uploader Privacy Policy

Updated October 12, 2026. This policy describes the FedReg Intel TikTok desktop uploader prototype and its intended sandbox use. It supplements the FedReg Intel website privacy policy.

Data used

With your authorization, Login Kit provides an app-specific account identifier, display name, and avatar URL under user.info.basic. The uploader displays the connected account's name so you can check the destination. TikTok issues access and refresh tokens. The app uses video.upload only to send the original video you select as a draft and to check the corresponding processing status.

Local handling and retention

The app binds to 127.0.0.1 on the owner's computer. Client credentials, authorization state, profile data, tokens, upload identifiers, and session-only file hashes are held in process memory; the app does not write them to files or GitHub. Your selected video's bytes pass through local memory to TikTok. The uploader does not delete or modify your source file. Closing the application clears its memory. The operating system or browser may separately manage cached files, process memory, and security logs.

TikTok processing

When you press Connect, credentials and authorization data are exchanged with TikTok. Only after your explicit per-video consent does the app send the selected video to TikTok. TikTok receives normal network/device information and retains content under its own policies. We do not control TikTok's retention or processing. See TikTok's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and tracking

The local uploader has no analytics, advertising trackers, marketing-email functions, or public user database. It does not sell account data. It requests no followers, comments, private messages, or other creators' videos. Its external API calls are to TikTok; links you choose to open use the linked services.

Disconnect, deletion, and revocation

Use Disconnect and revoke access in the local app to request revocation at TikTok; a successful response also clears app memory. If revocation fails, use TikTok's connected-app controls to remove access. Closing the uploader clears local session data but does not itself revoke the grant at TikTok or remove uploaded drafts. Delete drafts or posts in TikTok. The original video remains on your computer until you remove it.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or assistance, email hello@fedregintel.com. This owner-operated prototype is not a public account-management service. Update this policy before introducing persistent storage, hosted uploads, additional permissions, or public users.
