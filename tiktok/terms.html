FEDREG INTEL · TIKTOK DRAFT UPLOADER

Uploader Terms of Use

Updated October 12, 2026. These terms describe the owner-operated FedReg Intel TikTok draft uploader prototype. Production access and TikTok approval are pending.

Purpose

The uploader helps its authorized owner review original educational videos and transfer them to TikTok as drafts. Final editing, disclosures, visibility, and publication happen in TikTok. A transferred file or processing response is not a published-post confirmation.

Your responsibilities

  • Upload only material you own or have permission to use, including video, images, voices, audio, and music.
  • Review the facts and official source links. Do not upload unresolved fact-check issues or teasers paired with an unconfirmed YouTube release.
  • Check the connected account and approve each upload.
  • Apply required AI-generated-content and own-brand disclosures, then review your caption, privacy choice, and publishing decision in TikTok.
  • Follow TikTok's Terms, Community Guidelines, and Content Posting API requirements.

Restrictions

Do not use this app for impersonation, harassment, unauthorized reuse, misleading government affiliation, or bypassing TikTok's permissions or limits. This prototype does not provide unattended public posting, and it must not be represented to TikTok as an implemented public multi-creator service.

Availability and limits

TikTok can refuse, delay, restrict, or review uploads. The starter accepts local MP4 files up to 64 MiB. Upload history and duplicate protection exist only during the running app session. Check TikTok before retrying after a restart or uncertain result. No guarantee is made about API approval, delivery time, indexing, publication, audience, or revenue.

Security and privacy

Keep client secrets and tokens out of public repositories, recordings, and chat messages. Run the uploader on your own computer. See the uploader privacy policy for data handling and revocation.
