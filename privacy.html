FedReg Intel: Privacy Policy

Updated September 30, 2026

Email signup list

If you choose to join, we collect your email address, signup time, and the version of your consent to future FedReg Intel updates and book promotions. We store this information in a private Cloudflare database, not in our public GitHub repository. We do not sell the subscriber list.

Emails are currently on hold. Signing up does not send a welcome email or start a campaign. We retain signups until you ask us to remove them or we discontinue the list. To withdraw consent, request access, or request deletion, contact hello@fedregintel.com from the address you signed up with. Future marketing emails will include an unsubscribe option.

Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent automated signups and processes browser and connection information under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not add IP addresses to our subscriber records. Cloudflare may process service and security logs.

The public website

The rule dashboard requests public document information from FederalRegister.gov. Your search terms and normal connection information are sent to that service when you search. Your watchlist is stored in your browser's local storage, not in an account or our server. You can remove saved rules from the watchlist or clear this site's browser storage.

The website displays public YouTube titles, thumbnails and links. Loading thumbnails contacts YouTube's image servers; fonts load from Google Fonts. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and deliver the site and may process connection information under their own privacy policies. Amazon and YouTube links take you to those services. This website does not add analytics or advertising trackers.

The channel owner's publishing app

"FedReg Intel" is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the FedReg Intel YouTube channel (@FedRegIntel). It runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and no one other than the channel owner signs in to it.

What Google data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API with these permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload), to publish episodes to the FedReg Intel channel.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl), to set video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, check whether a video is public, and post a comment with links to the official documents discussed.

The app only acts on the FedReg Intel channel's own videos. It does not read, collect or store other people's data, including viewers' comments or personal information.

How data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's computer. The app has no servers or databases, and no data is sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API to carry out the actions above.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored token on the owner's computer also ends the app's access.
